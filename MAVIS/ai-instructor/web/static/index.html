How you're scored

Each area is scored 0–100 from your live flying, then combined into a weighted total. The debrief is written by MAVIS; the scores are quantitative.

Track, how closely your green line followed the grey reference path.
Speed, how well you held each leg's target speed.
Altitude, holding circuit height on downwind and a steady glide on final.
Landing, touchdown softness, on the centreline, at the right speed (weighted most).
Smoothness, gentle, unhurried control inputs.

Your total becomes a letter grade:

GradeABCDF
Total88+76+64+50+below 50

Retries (in Trainee and Pilot modes) cap your best possible total:

Retries01234+
Max total10085787060

In Visitor and Exam modes retries are shown but don't cap your score.

There's a rare top mark above A, you'll know it if you earn it.

Instruction
Jargon

Layman uses plain, beginner-friendly language ("put the flaps down"). Pilot uses standard aviation callouts ("select landing flap"). Visitor expects Layman; Trainee and Pilot expect Pilot, you'll be asked if they differ. Exam mode (below) expects Pilot.

Locked during training, change from the main menu before your next session.

Exam mode

When on, the next circuit is a silent assessment: no coaching, chat disabled, summary at the end. An Exam badge appears on the main menu and training screen.

Locked during training, change from the main menu before your next session.

Instructor tone

Harsh is blunt, no-nonsense coaching with strong language, for Trainee and Pilot sessions when you want corrections that hit hard.

Locked during training, change from the main menu before your next session.
